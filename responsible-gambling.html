Responsible Gambling

Gambling should stay fun. When it stops being fun, it's time to step back.

Need help right now? Reach out to one of the helplines listed below — they are free, confidential, and available 24/7 in most countries.

The basics

  • Set a deposit limit and stick to it.
  • Set a time limit. Use the operator's session-time tools.
  • Never gamble money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Don't chase losses.
  • Don't gamble when emotional, intoxicated, or under stress.
  • Take regular breaks.
  • Treat gambling spend as entertainment cost, not income.

Warning signs

Gambling may be becoming a problem if you find yourself:

  • Spending more than you planned, or borrowing to gamble.
  • Hiding gambling activity from people close to you.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when not gambling.
  • Gambling to escape stress or low mood.
  • Lying about losses, or chasing them with bigger bets.

Tools at the operator

Most licensed operators, including Golazzo, offer:

  • Deposit limits (daily, weekly, monthly)
  • Loss limits
  • Session-time limits
  • Reality-check pop-ups
  • Self-exclusion (temporary cool-off or longer-term lock-out)

You can request any of these in your account settings or by contacting customer support.

Where to get help

  • GamCare — UK helpline (0808 8020 133, 24/7)
  • BeGambleAware — UK information & signposting
  • Gamblers Anonymous — international peer support
  • National Council on Problem Gambling (US) — 1-800-GAMBLER
  • Gambling Help Online (Australia)
  • ConnexOntario (Canada)
  • Gamban — software to block gambling sites and apps

Underage gambling

Gambling is restricted to adults aged 18 and over (21+ in some jurisdictions). If you share a device with minors, use parental control software and password-protect your accounts.
